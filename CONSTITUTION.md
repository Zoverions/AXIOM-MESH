# AXIOM-MESH CONSTITUTION

**Build:** `0.12.0-dev.3`

**Status:** constitutional direction for the kernel-governed product and
network programme. Executable authority and public claims remain subordinate
to the signed policy stack, capability registry, normative requirements, and
reproducible evidence.

AXIOM-MESH exists to increase human and collective capability without creating
an unaccountable execution path. No founder, token, contract, model, browser,
operator, remote node, autonomous agent, settlement process, domain capsule, or
Grid service may bypass the Gateway → Hypervisor → Sandbox → Grid control loop
for a privileged or externally visible effect.

The project may build broadly. It must activate deliberately, expose minimally,
promote only with evidence, and market only what is true.

The following states are constitutionally distinct:

1. code that is **built**;
2. capability that is operator-**enabled**;
3. capability that is user- or network-**exposed**;
4. an exact build and deployment that are **production-promoted**;
5. scope that is publicly **marketed**.

Existence of code, a demonstration, synthetic evidence, a laboratory result, or
an operator toggle is not production promotion.

The present kernel is a single-node transparency log. It is not a live
blockchain government, BFT network, proof-of-truth system, public federation,
or mainnet economic deployment. It can authenticate constrained machine
principals, but that primitive does not constitute an autonomous-agent runtime,
agent federation, remote execution system, or delegated machine government.
Distributed authority, token, settlement, autonomous-agent, regulated-domain,
embodied-system, arbitrary-code, zk, and post-quantum work may proceed in
isolated laboratories. Laboratory work must remain disabled by default and
separated from production identities, secrets, user data, real value, and
public authority until separately ratified, implemented, tested, reviewed, and
promoted.

Human-facing products must preserve rather than conceal the authority model.
A friendly interface may simplify language, but it may not hide denial,
uncertainty, degraded state, unresolved conflict, external data transfer,
consequential cost, irreversibility, or the difference between evidence of
integrity and proof of truth. Accessibility, export, deletion, revocation,
appeal, and recovery are product safety requirements.

Policy inheritance is deny-dominant. Lower layers may reduce authority but may
not weaken a higher denial, lower a risk classification, replace an authorized
tool or provider, suppress consent, recovery, deletion, export, or appeal, or
expand their own power. Machine principals are additionally bounded by their
sponsor, finite scopes, action and purpose ceilings, runtime identity, expiry,
non-delegation, the currently enforced execution-time ceiling, and authenticated
Gateway request-size, request-rate, concurrency, and response-size ceilings,
and current built-in effects are additionally constrained by an AXIOM-computed
destination that must remain inside the principal's finite destination ceiling.
Machine discovery may expose only a principal-specific requestable intersection
of active policy and machine constraints; discovery, listing, installation, or
connection never creates permission. Machine constraints may reduce but never
enlarge policy authority.
Permitted high-risk effects require explicit approval from an independent
authenticated principal.

Every mutation must remain attributable, signed, hash-linked, exportable within
its lawful and consented scope, and subject to a visible recovery and dispute
path. Installation, discovery, invitation, connection, or payment never grants
execution authority. Missing providers, credentials, consent, verifiers,
destinations, settlement adapters, quorum, or required evidence fail closed.

Core access and identity must not depend on a token. Economic mechanisms must
remain subordinate to human authority, solvency, custody, dispute, rollback,
and independent audit. Social importance does not create special trust:
health, education, government, legal, employment, finance, minors, and embodied
systems require stricter—not weaker—consent, minimization, appeal, safety,
legal, and evidence boundaries.

Only capabilities marked `implemented` in
`mesh/config/capabilities.json` are runnable claims. The generated status and
release verifier bind this document to the registry schema, kernel version, and
digest so constitutional language cannot silently outrun the executable
system.
