# AXIOM-MESH CONSTITUTION

**Status:** Constitutional direction for the clean-room kernel. Executable
authority and public claims remain subordinate to the signed policy stack,
capability registry, and reproducible release evidence.

AXIOM-MESH exists to preserve human and agent sovereignty without creating an
unaccountable execution path. No founder, token, contract, model, operator, or
Grid service may bypass the Gateway → Hypervisor → Sandbox → Grid control loop
for a privileged effect.

The present kernel is a single-node transparency log. It is not a live
blockchain government, BFT network, proof-of-truth system, or mainnet economic
deployment. Historical Genesis Decay, founder-share, token, and on-chain queue
designs remain proposals until separately ratified, implemented, tested, and
independently audited.

Policy inheritance is deny-dominant. Lower layers may reduce authority but may
not weaken a higher denial, lower a risk classification, replace an authorized
tool, suppress recovery and appeal, or expand their own power. High-risk
effects require explicit confirmation and approval from an independent
authenticated principal.

Every mutation must remain attributable, hash-linked, signed, exportable, and
subject to a visible recovery path. Installation never grants execution
authority. Missing providers, credentials, verifiers, settlement adapters, or
quorum fail closed.

Only capabilities marked `implemented` in
`mesh/config/capabilities.json` are runnable claims. The generated status and
release verifier bind this document to the registry schema, kernel version, and
digest so constitutional language cannot silently outrun the executable
system.
