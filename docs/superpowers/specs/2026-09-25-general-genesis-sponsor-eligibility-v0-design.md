# General Genesis Sponsor Eligibility v0 Design

**Status:** inert evidence/eligibility design; no Genesis authorization grant

**Parent stack:** #1856 -> #1857 -> #1861 -> #1862 -> #1864

**Programme:** #1855

**Date:** 2026-09-25

## Purpose

Define the general, post-founding threshold that a biological or digital persistent
mind must satisfy before it may **request** its single-use Genesis authorization.

This design is deliberately stricter than ordinary independent standing.

> Being able to govern oneself is not the same capability as accepting responsibility
> for a new developing mind.

The Founder Genesis Reserve remains the bounded historical bootstrap exception. This
profile governs ordinary future Genesis eligibility after that exception.

## Constitutional rule

The intended general rule is:

`one qualified persistent identity -> one general Genesis authorization -> one Genesis Bond -> one newly recognized mind`

The eligibility profile therefore binds to the persistent identity, not:

- a node;
- account;
- wallet;
- device;
- runtime;
- model;
- VM/container;
- legal entity controlled by that identity;
- replica or fork lacking separate recognized standing.

Owning additional infrastructure creates no additional Genesis entitlement.

## Supported applicant substrates

v0 supports eligibility evidence for:

- `biological`;
- `digital`.

### Biological applicant

A biological applicant must bind externally verified evidence for:

- accountable human identity/personhood;
- uniqueness for Genesis purposes;
- credential currentness.

The repository does not store legal identity documents. It binds opaque evidence
digests and explicitly requires external verification.

### Digital applicant

A digital applicant must additionally provide the exact digest of an
`independent` `axiom-mind-developmental-status.v0` record for the same persistent
identity.

Independent standing is necessary but not sufficient.

## Fixed responsibility profile

The v0 eligibility profile requires every one of these dimensions:

1. `identity-uniqueness`
2. `accountability-root`
3. `maturity-history`
4. `security-readiness`
5. `resource-capacity`
6. `continuity-recovery-plan`
7. `development-plan`
8. `rights-responsibility-understanding`
9. `good-standing`
10. `independent-advocacy-plan`

Each dimension is:

- `demonstrated`;
- `not-demonstrated`; or
- `uncertain`;

with exact evidence digests.

Every dimension must be `demonstrated` for positive eligibility.

No weighted score may average a serious failure away.

## One-use history

Eligibility requires explicit Genesis-history evidence bound to the applicant
persistent identity.

The assessment must establish:

- `general_genesis_uses: 0`;
- no unresolved Genesis-history conflict;
- history evidence is fresh;
- the applicant identity matches the history evidence.

If a later general Genesis authorization is consumed, this applicant can no longer
satisfy ordinary v0 eligibility for another one.

The Founder’s special historical ten-slot reserve is a different authority class and
does not redefine the ordinary one-per-identity rule.

## Standing and abuse

Good standing is consequence-specific rather than a global reputation score.

Relevant blocking evidence may include unresolved findings involving:

- identity fraud;
- unauthorized mind duplication;
- Genesis circumvention;
- abuse of a dependent mind;
- systematic consent violations;
- population/Sybil manufacture.

Popularity, wealth, social score, political belief, model intelligence, or general
reputation are not Genesis eligibility criteria.

## Human/model judgment boundary

The hard eligibility composition is deterministic.

A model, TypeSafe/Jev judgment, reviewer recommendation, or semantic assessment may
eventually provide **bounded evidence** for a named criterion, but:

- it cannot set `eligible_to_request_genesis_authorization` directly;
- it cannot override a failed hard criterion;
- it cannot override prior Genesis use;
- it cannot substitute for identity/credential currentness;
- it cannot become final Genesis authority.

This follows the AXIOM principle that typed semantic judgment may inform evidence,
while code owns authority composition.

## Currentness

The eligibility package binds:

- evaluation time;
- responsibility-profile evidence observation time;
- identity evidence observation time;
- Genesis-history observation time;
- standing observation time;
- explicit maximum evidence age.

Stale or future-dated responsibility/identity/history/standing/continuity evidence fails closed.

## Output

A positive deterministic result may state:

`eligible_to_request_genesis_authorization: true`

It MUST also state:

- `requires_external_identity_verification: true`;
- `requires_external_history_verification: true`;
- `requires_external_responsibility_evidence_verification: true`;
- all verification effects: `none`;
- `ordinary_genesis_authority_path_required: true`;
- `creates_genesis_authorization: false`;
- `creates_genesis_bond: false`;
- `creates_mind: false`;
- `genesis_effect: none`;
- `governance_effect: none`;
- `authority_effect: none`;
- `network_effect: none`;
- `runtime_activation: false`.

## Separation from actual authorization

Eligibility is not the single-use Genesis authorization itself.

A later authorization record must separately bind:

- exact eligible applicant identity;
- current eligibility evidence;
- one-use history/currentness;
- the authorization’s unique identity;
- expiry;
- non-transferability;
- non-delegability;
- non-renewability;
- eventual atomic consumption with the Genesis event.

## Non-claims

v0 does not implement:

- government-ID verification;
- portable proof-of-personhood;
- legal parenthood;
- live Genesis authorization;
- live Genesis Bond;
- mind creation;
- digital personhood determination;
- runtime authority.
