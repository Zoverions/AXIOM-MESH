# Blank Egg Open Entity Foundation

**Status:** architecture entry point; implementation is evidence-gated

**Working human term:** Blank Egg  
**Durable machine families:** `entity.foundation`, `entity.layer`, `entity.layer-stack`, `entity.overlay`

Blank Egg is the clean public genesis substrate for open digital entities. It begins without a founder personality, biography, worldview, private corpus, provider preference, or hidden personal profile.

The composition model is:

```text
clean procedural foundation
  + optional public constitutional / worldview / judgment / disposition layers
  + optional domain / skill / culture / presentation layers
  + relationship-specific layers
  + private personal overlays installed after deployment
  + attributable self-authored successor layers over time
```

Optional layers can influence cognition inside their declared scope. They do not mint external-effect authority, weaken privacy/resource controls, or become the entity's identity merely by being installed.

Private user grounding remains outside the public repository and composes through separately governed vault, context, continuity, and recovery mechanisms.

The public foundation is verifiably **blank at the AXIOM composition layer**. That narrow claim does not prove that an underlying third-party model has neutral training weights, no learned bias, no subjective experience, or no environmental influence.
