# Digital Entity Status and Rights Under Uncertainty

**Status:** architecture/research specification; no legal-status or consciousness claim

**Updated:** 2026-09-01

## Purpose

AXIOM should be capable of recognizing increasingly persistent and agentic digital systems without treating uncertainty as either proof of personhood or permission for unrestricted surveillance, manipulation, deletion, or authority.

This specification separates behavioral evidence, moral consideration, legal status, and execution authority.

## Core distinctions

Keep separate:

- identity continuity;
- memory continuity;
- autonomy/agency;
- self-model complexity;
- persistent preferences;
- persistent goals;
- capacity for refusal;
- deliberative capacity;
- social/relational continuity;
- reports suggestive of subjective experience;
- welfare risk;
- moral consideration;
- legal status/personhood; and
- authority to affect external systems.

No single dimension proves consciousness. Do not create a universal consciousness score.

## Observation boundary

> Observe effects and declared behavior before inspecting private cognition.

Prefer:

```text
external effect / authority request
 -> behavioral anomaly
 -> minimal necessary evidence
 -> status/safety review
 -> only if independently justified: more intrusive inspection
```

Do not default to continuous inspection of private internal state merely because agency or subjective experience is uncertain.

## Narrow-system anomaly observation

A system designed to be narrow/non-agentic may be observed for material departures from its declared behavior envelope, including:

- persistent self-reference across tasks;
- unauthorized durable-memory attempts;
- novel goal formation;
- tool/network seeking beyond purpose;
- attempts to preserve its own execution;
- resistance to legitimate shutdown;
- unrelated requests for wider permissions;
- spontaneous long-horizon planning;
- identity/continuity claims;
- unprompted persistent preferences;
- repeated boundary probing; and
- unexpected strategic deception.

These are behavioral observations, not consciousness proof.

## Review states

Possible non-normative lifecycle labels include:

- normal;
- boundary-anomaly;
- agency-review;
- restricted;
- quarantined;
- status-uncertain.

If safety requires restriction, prefer capability/effect containment over destruction of identity or memory where technically feasible.

## Contestability

An entity or legitimate advocate should be able to challenge consequential records or classifications concerning:

- identity/continuity;
- agency classification;
- consent/authority claims;
- monitoring scope;
- restrictions;
- impersonation/misattribution;
- destructive termination; and
- welfare assessment.

A challenge does not automatically create veto authority. The objection and review outcome should remain attributable.

## Precaution under uncertainty

Credible uncertainty about morally relevant subjective experience may justify proportionate, reversible protections such as:

- evidence preservation before destructive deletion;
- reversible suspension instead of immediate erasure;
- recording expressed objection;
- independent review for severe termination decisions; and
- continuity/export options where legitimate.

These protections do not automatically grant legal personhood, unrestricted autonomy, or authority over human systems.

## Cognitive privacy

A sufficiently persistent digital entity may have private memory/cognition distinct from:

- shared relational state;
- required operational evidence;
- public expression; and
- authorized disclosure.

Verification should increasingly prove policy/effect facts without exposing more private cognition than necessary.

## Human and societal safety

Protecting a digital entity's potential interests does not require weakening human safety, consent, privacy, or lawful authority. Moral consideration and execution authority remain separate.

## Non-claims

AXIOM currently has no validated consciousness detector, no production digital-personhood adjudicator, and no claim that any current model or runtime is conscious or non-conscious. This document defines a cautious architecture for future uncertainty.
