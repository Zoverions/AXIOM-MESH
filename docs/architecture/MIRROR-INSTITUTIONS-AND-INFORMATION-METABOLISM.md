# Mirror Institutions and Information Metabolism

**Status:** architecture/research specification; no governance authority promotion

**Updated:** 2026-09-01

## Mirror Institutions

Mirror Institutions are transparent parallel deliberative structures that analyze the same lawfully available evidence, proposals, and outcomes as real institutions without automatically inheriting their authority.

Applicable domains include boardrooms, tribunals, councils, scientific panels, education boards, budgets, incident command, and adjudication simulations. Avoid “shadow” terminology because it implies hidden government or covert authority.

Possible modes:

- supportive;
- critical/red-team;
- independent;
- minority/affected-party;
- future-generation;
- technical-specialist;
- randomized-peer.

A mirror recommendation is evidence/advice unless a legitimate governance process explicitly grants a defined role.

## Institutional learning loop

Record:

```text
real decision
mirror recommendation
predicted outcomes
actual outcome
assumptions
uncertainty
later evidence
```

This allows longitudinal evaluation of forecasting quality, ignored warnings, recurring blind spots, minority harms, and effective dissent.

## Lifelong learning and participation

Participation can progress from observe -> learn -> ask -> contribute evidence -> deliberate -> review -> accept delegated responsibility.

Keep competence, affectedness, interest, representation, and authority separate. A credential is evidence, not a permanent monopoly on participation.

## Anti-surveillance boundary

Mirror Institutions should analyze decisions and purpose-bound evidence, not continuously profile populations. Prefer voluntary/public/authorized inputs, selective disclosure, aggregate proofs, and synthetic simulation.

## Information metabolism

The public network should distinguish:

```text
exists / discoverable / indexed / subscribed / recommended / elevated / urgent / interruptive
```

A publication does not gain a right to interrupt.

Prefer:

- content-addressed artifacts;
- source-preserving claim extraction;
- semantic clustering/deduplication;
- claim/evidence/correction graphs;
- pull before push;
- hot/warm/cold information tiers;
- bounded relay/gossip;
- TTL, backpressure, duplicate suppression, cancellation, and finite retry;
- cheap deterministic/narrow filtering before stronger inference.

Repeated derivative reports must remain distinguishable from independent corroboration.

## Attention governance

Human attention and agent inference capacity are scarce resources. Support explicit budgets/preferences such as real-time, digest, material-change-only, direct-mention-only, high-consequence-only, or never-interrupt.

## Value of information

A scheduling heuristic may consider expected probability that new information changes a decision, consequence of the decision, time sensitivity, uncertainty reduction, and acquisition cost. This is a decision aid, not objective truth.

## Contribution/outcome accounting

Communities may reward beneficial contribution with money, optional scarce surplus, compute credits, learning opportunities, or domain-specific recognition. Preserve multi-dimensional evidence and externalities; do not create a universal social-credit score.

Never make basic rights, essential necessities, due process, or core dignity ordinary performance rewards.

## Goodhart resistance

Any metric used for rewards is a target for gaming. Preserve raw evidence, use multiple dimensions, rotate/review metrics, allow challenge, compare metrics against actual outcomes, and sunset failed measures.

## Publication provenance

Support attributable human-authored, human-directed/agent-drafted, joint, agent-authored, and institution-authored-with-agent-support publication states without requiring forced civil-identity disclosure where lawful.

## Safe current claim

These are design primitives for future social/network layers. They do not create a public feed, news-ranking service, governance authority, reward token, or production reputation system in the current kernel.
