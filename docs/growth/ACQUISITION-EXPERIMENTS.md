## Operating rules

- Carry forward only experiments that produce useful visits, tests, activation, referrals, contributions, or high-quality technical feedback.
- Rotate audience, channel, format, and call-to-action rather than reposting the same message.
- Prefer active technical conversations and real project artifacts over generic promotion.
- Never weaken authentication, authorization, provenance, consent, fail-closed behavior, or privacy controls for conversion.
- Do not claim a capability is production-promoted when the capability registry says otherwise.
- Keep campaign tracking non-invasive; use campaign codes or ordinary UTM parameters only where the destination supports them without changing trust or privacy semantics.
