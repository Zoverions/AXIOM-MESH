# AXIOM MESH Network Service Charter — One-Page Public Version

**Version:** v1.0-draft  
**Date:** 2026-04-08  
**Audience:** Partners, builders, operators, communities, and public stakeholders.

---

## Mission
AXIOM MESH provides cross-network infrastructure and trust services that help digital societies flourish while protecting dignity, safety, and user agency.

## What We Offer
- Interoperable infrastructure services across partner chains/platforms.
- Verifiable trust services (attestations, policy checks, evidence trails).
- Governance-aware orchestration for high-impact actions.

## Operating Principle
**Portable utility, anchored trust:**
- Utility and APIs are designed for broad integration.
- Trust-critical guarantees are rooted in AXIOM MESH governance and verification.

## Economic Model
AXIOM MESH sustains itself through:
1. Managed infrastructure and reliability SLAs.
2. Premium trust services and policy assurance.
3. Governance and dispute tooling for high-impact workflows.

We do **not** monetize through hidden lock-in, opaque preference, or extractive access controls.

## Fairness & Ethics Commitments
- Transparent policy and fee logic.
- Non-discriminatory baseline access.
- Appeal pathways for contested high-impact decisions.
- Human-readable and machine-verifiable evidence logs.
- Public-good reinvestment into core infrastructure and ecosystem tooling.

## Human + Digital Participation
AXIOM MESH supports participation by biological and digital actors.
Rights and responsibilities are assigned by role, risk, and impact domain (biological, digital, or mixed).

## Automation + Governance
- **Automatic by default** for low-risk, reversible operations.
- **Governance required** for high-impact, rights-affecting, or irreversible changes.
- Emergency controls are time-bounded, auditable, and subject to retrospective review.

## Integration Promise to Partners
- Open integration interfaces.
- Clear trust and risk assumptions.
- Domain-aware policy overlays (global baseline + national + local/business constraints).
- No silent policy bypass for privileged integrators.

## Accountability
AXIOM MESH tracks and publishes outcomes across:
- Reliability (uptime/latency/incidents)
- Economic sustainability (recurring revenue and concentration risk)
- Governance quality (participation, appeal turnaround, capture resistance)
- Ethical integrity (fairness audits and policy exception trends)

## Public Commitment
We will scale revenue in ways that preserve constitutional integrity, maintain contestability, and improve collective capacity—not merely extract value.
