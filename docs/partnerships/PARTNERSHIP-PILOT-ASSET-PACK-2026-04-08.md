## 1) Executive Positioning

AXIOM is positioned as an augmentation and assurance layer, not a replacement chain/cloud.

- **Render augmentation:** verified external compute with signed evidence, deterministic policy gating, and fail-closed settlement.
- **Polkadot augmentation:** controlled interoperability and staged activation via explicit trust boundaries and governance gates.

Outcome: higher confidence cross-network execution with measurable reliability, safety, and governance auditability.

## 3) Pilot Scope Definition

## 3.1 Pilot objective
Validate that AXIOM can orchestrate cross-network AI workflows with auditable evidence and policy enforcement while maintaining predictable economics and operational resilience.

## 3.2 Duration and phases
- **Phase A (2 weeks):** sandbox integration and synthetic workloads.
- **Phase B (3 weeks):** controlled production-like workloads, limited partner set.
- **Phase C (1 week):** governance closure, scorecard review, go/no-go recommendation.

## 3.3 Included capabilities
1. Render adapter submission + callback ingestion.
2. ZKML attestation envelope verification path.
3. Polkadot integration control plane simulation (sender/receiver boundaries).
4. Cross-network settlement logging (fee split, burn, escrow holdback).
5. Incident drill: failed attestation, delayed callback, and route isolation.

## 3.4 Explicit exclusions
1. Mainnet bridge value transfer.
2. Unlimited public onboarding.
3. Irreversible governance parameter changes.

## 4) Pilot Success Metrics

## 4.1 Reliability and performance
- Job completion success rate: **>= 99.0%** in pilot workload set.
- P95 callback-to-verification latency: **<= 120 seconds**.
- Route failover recovery (degraded mode): **<= 5 minutes**.

## 4.2 Evidence and security
- Evidence completeness (jobs with full receipt chain): **>= 99.5%**.
- Invalid attestation acceptance rate: **0** tolerated.
- Signed callback verification coverage: **100%**.

## 4.3 Governance and risk
- Referenda with complete accountability packet: **100%** for pilot decisions.
- High-risk partner actions lacking mitigation plan: **0** tolerated.
- Incident drill completion with recorded lessons: **100%**.

## 4.4 Economic outcomes
- Settlement variance (expected vs observed): **<= 0.5%** per epoch.
- Deferred burn/mint events outside policy caps: **0** tolerated.
- Unit-cost predictability drift vs quote: **<= 8%** at P95.

## 7) Go/No-Go Rubric

**Go** when all are true:
1. Reliability/security/economic KPI thresholds are met.
2. No unresolved high-severity settlement variances.
3. Governance closure packet approved with mitigation owners assigned.

**Conditional Go** when:
- KPIs are met except minor non-security deviations, and corrective plan has bounded timeline.

**No-Go** when any of:
- Evidence completeness below threshold.
- Any accepted invalid attestation.
- Unmitigated high/critical partnership risk.
