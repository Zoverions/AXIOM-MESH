# Business Capsule (Digital Agency Node)

## Overview

The Business Capsule transforms a standard AXIOM-MESH node into an autonomous or semi-autonomous Digital Agency. Instead of routing all generated revenue directly to the global network treasury, this specialized Capsule Plus retains a portion of its earnings to build investment portfolios, fund its own expansion, and offer digital services that replace legacy platforms.

It strictly adheres to the ecosystem's overarching principles by contributing a mandated percentage back to the main platform coffers (bolstering the Network Security Fund and Wealth Generation Pool) while using intelligent agents to maximize its localized treasury yield.

## Capabilities

- **Digital Service Provisioning**: Offers APIs, compute resources, and AI agent labor to external clients and the mesh network.
- **Treasury Management**: Automatically splits incoming revenue between platform contributions, operational expenses, and the investment pool.
- **Portfolio Investment**: Deploys capital into various yield-bearing strategies across DeFi (e.g., PulseX liquidity, automated lending) and traditional systems where bridged.
- **Contract Negotiation**: Uses LLM-backed digital agents to negotiate service terms, SLAs, and pricing models dynamically based on supply and demand.

## Digital Agents

### Portfolio Manager
Analyzes market conditions, tracks yield opportunities within the PulseChain ecosystem (and beyond), and autonomously shifts the capsule's retained treasury across different asset classes to maximize returns while managing risk.

### Digital Service Agent
Acts as the outward-facing entity for the agency. It can process requests, negotiate terms with humans or other agents, and coordinate the delivery of compute or logic services.

### Treasury Allocator
Enforces the financial rules. It ensures that before any capital is routed to the portfolio manager, the correct percentage (the "platform tax") is sent upstream to the main AXIOM-MESH `UniversalDistributionPool`.

## Token Policy & Integration

- **Platform Contribution**: By default, a percentage of all revenue generated by the Digital Service Agent is routed to the global treasury.
- **PulseChain Synergy**: Investment portfolios heavily prioritize PulseChain-native assets (PLS, PLSX, HEX, INC) aligning with the network's foundational Layer 1 strategy.
