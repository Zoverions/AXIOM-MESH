# AXIOM-MESH Health & Longevity Capsule

This Capsule provides the foundational logic for integrating deterministic unit privacy with individual health data control. Its primary goals are longevity analysis, cause-and-effect tracking, and quality of life enhancement.

## Core Principles
- **Deterministic Unit Privacy:** Ensuring health data is processed privately and securely.
- **Individual Control:** Users have absolute sovereignty over their health data and who can access it.
- **Longevity and Quality of Life:** Leveraging data to provide actionable insights for better health outcomes.

## Capabilities
- Privacy-Preserving Data Analysis
- Longevity Tracking
- Cause and Effect Analysis
- Quality of Life Metrics

This capsule seamlessly integrates into the AXIOM-MESH ecosystem, operating autonomously to assist users in their health journeys while adhering to strict privacy constraints.
